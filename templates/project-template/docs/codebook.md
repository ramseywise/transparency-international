## 3. Outcomes

Every classification ends in one of three outcomes. They are different, and each has its own requirement.

| Outcome | Means | Required |
|---|---|---|
| positive | the evidence shows the case meets the definition | source, quote |
| negative | the evidence shows the case does not meet the definition | source and quote showing what it is instead; what was searched |
| unknown | the evidence does not settle it | the reason: nothing found, sources conflict, source unavailable, search failed |

A search that finds nothing gives "unknown", never "negative".
