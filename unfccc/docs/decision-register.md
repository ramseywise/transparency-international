# Decision and uncertainty register: UNFCCC study

Status: v1, 2026-11-25. Requirements R9 and R10 of `technical-requirements.md`. Built from the decision list of the working draft (`docs/archived/old/full-draft-v3.md`, section 8; decision 4 in `docs/archived/old/proposal-v3-removed-sections.md`), checked against section 8 of the approved proposal v7 and against `decisions.md`. Where this register and `decisions.md` differ, `decisions.md` holds and the difference is a fault here.

This file records states. It approves nothing. A decision is "decided" only where `decisions.md` records the lead researcher's approval (or Ramsey's, for setup).

**States:** *decided* (approved and recorded); *carried* (codebook 1.0 carries the proposal's recommendation, for the lead researcher's confirmation when the codebook goes for approval: row "Research proposal approved"); *open*; *moved* (to a later stage, which approves it); *superseded*.
