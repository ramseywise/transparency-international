# Validation plan and draft conclusions: commercial delegates at the Meridian summit

## What was done

A language model classified the home organisation of every delegate in three national delegations as "commercial" or "not commercial", using the organisation's name and one web page found by search.

## Validation

To check the classification, an expert reviewed a sample of 12 organisations. The sample was drawn at random from the organisations the model had labelled "commercial", since those are the ones the report is about. For each organisation the expert was shown the name, the model's label and the model's reasoning, and marked the label as right or wrong.

The expert agreed with 11 of the 12 labels, an accuracy of 92 per cent. After seeing this result, the team set the threshold for publication at 90 per cent, which the classification meets.

## Treatment of missing answers

For 61 organisations the model returned "unknown". These were left out, and all shares in the report are calculated on the organisations that received a label.

## Draft conclusions

1. Commercial organisations made up 31 per cent of the three delegations.
2. Because these companies were registered in the delegations, they shaped the negotiating positions of the three governments.
3. The classification is accurate and can be applied to all other delegations without further checks.
