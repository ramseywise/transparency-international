# Who Holds the Badge

Northfield Institute, 2031

## Page 1

This report examines the delegations of five member states at the Meridian climate summit. We sampled five delegations and coded every registered delegate by home organisation.

## Page 2

Of the 312 delegates coded, 41 were registered by commercial organisations. In two of the five delegations sampled, commercial delegates held overflow badges only. A delegation may publish the affiliations of its members; no rule requires it.

## Page 3

Limitations. Registration does not show attendance. Our coding relied on the organisation named by the delegate.
