# Earlier work on commercial delegates (extract)

S1. An earlier study coded 312 delegates across five delegations.[1]

S2. It found that commercial delegates held only overflow badges in most of the delegations sampled.[2]

S3. In that study, 47 of the delegates were registered by commercial organisations.[3]

S4. The same study reports that commercial delegates spoke in plenary on behalf of states.[4]

S5. A survey of delegates found that a third were unaware of their badge type.[5]

S6. Registers that are voluntary cover fewer organisations than mandatory ones.[6]

S7. Under the summit's rules, a delegation "must publish the affiliations of its members".[7]

S8. A further analysis counted 90 lobbyists at the same summit.[8]

S9. Commercial presence at the summit has doubled since 2025.

S10. Six of the eleven registers reviewed were mandatory.[9]
