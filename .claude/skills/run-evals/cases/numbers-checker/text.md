# Delegations at the Meridian summit: summary of findings

N1. The file holds 40 registrations across three delegations.

N2. Alba registered 16 delegates, Borea 14 and Cestia 10.

N3. In all, 14 delegates were registered by business organisations.

N4. Most of Borea's delegates came from business.

N5. In Cestia, business accounts for 30 per cent of the delegation (3 of 10).

N6. Party overflow badges account for 55 per cent of all registrations (22 of 40).

N7. Alba registered 4 business delegates.

N8. About 250 organisations were represented.

N9. Civil society organisations registered 10 delegates, 25 per cent of the total.
