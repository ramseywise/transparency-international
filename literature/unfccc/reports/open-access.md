Of 193 sources screened in without a file: 50 have a legal open PDF, 12 an open web page but no direct PDF, 18 are known to OpenAlex with no open copy, 13 could not be matched, and 100 were not looked up (reports and web pages OpenAlex does not list, or the title search limit).

An open copy may be the author's manuscript rather than the journal version: its page numbers differ, so a quote must cite the version actually read.
